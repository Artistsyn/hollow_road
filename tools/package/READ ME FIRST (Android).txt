HOLLOW ROAD - Android test build
================================

Needs an Android phone or tablet on Android 13 or newer with a 64-bit (arm64) processor, which
is nearly every phone from the last several years. It plays in landscape. Nothing else to install.

1. Copy "Hollow Road.apk" to the phone (a cable, a cloud drive, or a message to yourself), and
   tap it in the Files app (or wherever it landed).

2. The first time only: this build does not come from the Play Store, so Android asks whether
   the app you tapped it in may install apps. Choose Settings, turn on "Allow from this source",
   go back and tap Install. You can turn that setting off again afterwards.
   If Play Protect warns that it does not recognise the app, choose "More details" and
   "Install anyway". It is a test build signed for testing, not a published one.

3. Open Hollow Road from the app drawer. Pick a pilgrimage slot, then a calling:
     Wayfarer     sword and buckler: rolls, parries, ripostes
     Warden       a tall bulwark: blocks fill a ward; a block at the last moment bursts it
     Bloodrunner  a bow whose arrows cost health
     Frostweaver  a frost focus (a catalyst) whose spell charges are filled by fighting up close
   then whether to play the prologue first: a short remembered fight that teaches walking,
   strikes, guarding, rolling and parrying with their timing, a flask, and a side-on bout.

TOUCH CONTROLS
  Left half of the screen   a stick: put your thumb down anywhere there and push from that
                            spot. Ahead walks on, back walks back, sideways steps a lane; push
                            far to run. In side-on fights it moves you left and right.
  Right half                the buttons, with STRIKE under your thumb where it rests:
                            STRIKE, HEAVY, ROLL, GUARD, PARRY, FLASK, SHOOT, CAST, ART,
                            JUMP (side-on), USE (rest, talk, open), TURN, MAP, II (pause)
  Menus and cutscenes       tap a choice to pick it; tap a cutscene to skip it.
  Warning colours over an enemy:  gold = parry it,  orange = roll or block,  purple = roll

UPDATING
  Install a newer "Hollow Road.apk" over this one the same way: your saves are kept. Uninstalling
  the app deletes its saves.

SAVES
  Your progress is kept automatically, every few seconds, in up to five slots. Carrying a
  pilgrimage on wakes you at the last shrine you rested at.
