HOLLOW ROAD - test build
========================

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or newer. Nothing else to install.

1. Unzip, and drag "Hollow Road.app" into Applications (or anywhere you like).

2. The first time only: this build is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS
   will say it cannot be opened. Either:
     - double-click it, press Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down
       to the message about "Hollow Road" and press "Open Anyway" (then confirm); or
     - in Terminal, run:   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Hollow Road.app"
       and then double-click it as usual.
   After that it opens normally.

3. Pick a pilgrimage slot, then a calling:
     Wayfarer     sword and buckler: rolls, parries, ripostes
     Warden       a tall bulwark: blocks fill a ward; a block at the last moment bursts it
     Bloodrunner  a bow whose arrows cost health
     Frostweaver  a frost focus whose charges are filled by fighting up close

CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse)
  On the road     W / S walk     A / D roll     Shift run     Space backstep
                  Left click strike     Shift + left click heavy
                  Right click guard     Shift + right click parry
                  R drink     F loose an arrow or bolt     E rest / talk / use
                  T turn back     M map     Esc back out of a menu
  Side-on fights  A / D move     Space + A / D roll     W jump (same attacks)
  Warning colours over an enemy:  gold = parry it,  orange = roll or block,  purple = roll

SAVES
  Your progress is kept automatically, every few seconds, in up to five slots. Carrying a
  pilgrimage on wakes you at the last shrine you rested at. Saves live in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Hollow Road/profiles (delete that folder to start clean).
