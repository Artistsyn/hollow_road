HOLLOW ROAD - test build
========================

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or newer. Nothing else to install.

1. Unzip, and drag "Hollow Road.app" into Applications (or anywhere you like).

2. The first time only: this build is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS
   will say it cannot be opened. Either:
     - double-click it, press Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down
       to the message about "Hollow Road" and press "Open Anyway" (then confirm); or
     - in Terminal, run:   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Hollow Road.app"
       and then double-click it as usual.
   After that it opens normally.

3. Pick a pilgrimage slot, then a calling:
     Wayfarer     sword and buckler: rolls, parries, ripostes
     Warden       a tall bulwark: blocks fill a ward; a block at the last moment bursts it
     Bloodrunner  a bow whose arrows cost health
     Frostweaver  a frost focus (a catalyst) whose spell charges are filled by fighting up close
   then whether to play the prologue first: a short remembered fight on a moonlit road that
   teaches walking, strikes, guarding, rolling and parrying with their timing, a flask, and a
   side-on bout. Nothing of the story is lost by skipping it, and Esc leaves it at any time.

CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse)
  On the road     W / S walk     tap W twice and hold it to run
                  A / D step a lane across (free, but no dodge in it)
                  Shift + W / A / S / D roll that way (costs stamina, dodges blows)
                  Left click strike     Shift + left click heavy
                  Right click guard     Shift + right click parry
                  R drink     F ranged attack (bow, crossbow)     C cast a spell
                  Q weapon art (a weapon forged from a boss's remnant)
                  E rest / talk / use
                  T turn back (the way back meets every fork you passed)
                  M map     Esc pause / back out of a menu (Q also backs out of a menu)
                  H show or hide these controls on screen (hidden at first)
  Side-on fights  A / D move     tap A or D twice and hold it to run
                  Shift + A / D roll     W or Space jump (same attacks)
  Spells          cast with C through a catalyst held in the other hand (no shield then);
                  a catalyst casts its own spell, or one found and attuned in the Spell
                  place at a shrine. Two blows up close fill a charge.
                  Frost Bolt chills what it hits for four seconds: a third slower, and its
                  stance stays cracked
  Blood weapons   blood bows and crossbows (F) are drawn from your own health
  Weapon arts     each weapon the smith forges from a boss's remnant has an art of its own (Q;
                  ART on a touch screen), such as the Bell-Hammer's Toll, a slam that throws
                  everything near off balance, or the Chieftain's Cleaver thrown and caught
                  again. Arts are paid from the blue meter under your stamina, which fills
                  as you land blows, parry, and slip or block a blow at the last moment.
  Scaling         weapons, bows, catalysts and spells need levels (! in the shrine's list
                  marks one you lack) and grow with Might, Dexterity and Focus by their
                  letter grades; short of a weapon's needs it hits weakly, short of a
                  spell's it cannot be cast. Crossbows hit the same whatever your levels.
  Warning colours over an enemy:  gold = parry it,  orange = roll or block,  purple = roll

THE ROAD
  Enemies idle until they notice you. You need not fight them all: one in another lane can be
  walked or run past (it steps across to stop you, so step aside late, or run). Once past, an
  alert enemy comes after you; nothing faces behind you, so a blow from there is only dodged
  by a roll, and a glow rising from the bottom of the screen shows who is closing and what is
  coming, in its warning colour. They give up if you get far enough ahead.
  Some ways on are held: a keeper (or, in a gauntlet, every enemy) must fall before the road
  goes on, and whatever you ran past will catch up with you there.
  A way you cannot take yet is shut across its mouth: a chained gate wants a key, a barred door
  opens only from its far side (a shortcut). Once opened it stands open, and you walk through.
  T turns you round where you stand; everything stays where it was.
  Cutscenes (the opening of a new pilgrimage, each boss's entrance, the end) can be skipped
  with Esc or E.
  Sound and music: see assets/sfx/CREDITS.txt and assets/music/CREDITS.txt (all free/CC0).

SAVES
  Your progress is kept automatically, every few seconds, in up to five slots. Carrying a
  pilgrimage on wakes you at the last shrine you rested at. Saves live in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Hollow Road/profiles (delete that folder to start clean).
